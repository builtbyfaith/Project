Photography

Photography

The Challenge

Capture authentic moments across three distinct disciplines — portrait, basketball, and track — each requiring a different approach to composition, timing, and lighting to communicate mood, emotion, and athletic energy through still images.

The Solution

Natural lighting and environmental framing anchor the portrait work; decisive timing and strategic contrast drive the basketball coverage; and controlled framing with motion emphasis defines the track photography. Across all three, strong composition and clarity of subject ensure each image reads with visual impact and intention.

01 — Portrait Photography · People & Nature

People & Nature

A collection of portrait photographs exploring mood, personality, and environment through natural light and thoughtful composition — capturing both people and surroundings in a way that feels intentional and visually balanced.

Twelve portraits spanning candid black-and-white editorial, golden-hour outdoor lifestyle, moody beach and forest atmospheres, and intimate detail shots — natural lighting and environmental context are used throughout to communicate mood and personality. Each frame balances the subject with its surroundings to create images that feel both intentional and authentic.

02 — Basketball Photography

Gilbert High School — Game Coverage

A full game coverage shoot for Gilbert High School basketball — capturing energy, precision, and athletic emotion through decisive framing and strong composition.

Fifteen frames covering the full spectrum of game-day storytelling — tight player portraits, bench culture, in-game action sequences, wide court compositions, and scoreboard/environmental details. Strategic framing and contrast emphasize intensity, and timing throughout captures the pace and emotion of the game from multiple vantage points.

03 — Track Photography

Mesa College — Track & Field Meet

Track and field meet coverage for Mesa College — capturing motion and athletic performance to visually communicate speed, discipline, and competitive energy.

Eight frames from a Mesa College track meet — wide stadium crowd shots, tight starting-block compositions, mid-stride action captures, and candid pre-race moments. Controlled framing, timing, and lighting emphasize motion and athletic posture throughout, with careful attention to contrast and composition guiding the viewer's eye across each dynamic frame.
